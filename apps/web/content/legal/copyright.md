# Copyright and Takedown Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - If you think something on Duna infringes your copyright, send a notice to our designated agent.
> - If your content was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice.
> - We close the accounts of repeat infringers.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to content on duna.coach, the Duna apps, Duna HQ and club websites hosted by Duna. It follows the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

## 3. Sending a notice

Your notice must include:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the copyrighted work you say is infringed;
3. the material you say infringes, and where it is on Duna (a link is best);
4. your name, address, phone number and email;
5. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

## 4. What we do

We remove or disable access to the material promptly, notify the person who posted it, and give them a copy of the notice.

## 5. Counter-notice

If your material was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification, send our agent:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. the material removed and where it was;
3. a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and phone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your address (or, if outside the US, any district where Duna may be found), and that you will accept service from the person who sent the notice.

We send the counter-notice to the person who sent the notice. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action, we restore the material within 10 to 14 business days.

## 6. Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of people who repeatedly infringe, in appropriate circumstances.

## 7. Misrepresentation

Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can make you liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

## 8. Other complaints

For trademark, privacy or likeness complaints, email legal@duna.coach.
