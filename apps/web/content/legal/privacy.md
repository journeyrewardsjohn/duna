# Duna Privacy Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - We collect what we need to run your account, bookings, payments, ratings, video, messages and the features you turn on.
> - We share information with the clubs you deal with, with the service providers that run Duna for us, and when the law requires. We do not sell your information or use it for targeted advertising.
> - Health data has its own policy and is never used for advertising or AI.
> - Juniors' profiles and videos are never public. Children under 13 use Duna only through a guardian.
> - You can see, export, correct or delete your information from Account Settings, or by emailing privacy@duna.coach.

## 3. How we use it

- **To provide the Service:** accounts, profiles, family links, Bookings, payments, refunds, Credits, memberships, messages, video, live scores, maps, weather, calendars and notifications.
- **To calculate Sand Rating** and show results and rankings, and to keep a record of where results came from.
- **To keep people safe:** guardian inclusion in Junior conversations, safety screening of messages involving Juniors, reports and blocks, and preventing fraud and misuse.
- **To provide AI features** you use, as described in section 6.
- **To support you** and communicate with you about your account and Bookings.
- **To improve the Service**, using de-identified or aggregated information where we can.
- **To comply with law**, enforce our terms, and handle disputes, tax and accounting.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for targeted (cross-context behavioural) advertising. We do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you based solely on automated processing.

## 4. Who we share it with

4.1 **Organizations you deal with.** When you book, join, register or message an Organization, it receives the information it needs: your name, contact details, Booking details, waivers, forms, attendance and payments. If you share Health data with a coach or club, they receive what you chose for as long as you chose.

4.2 **Other users,** according to your settings: your public profile, results and public videos; your name to teammates, partners and opponents; your messages to the people you message. Juniors' profiles and videos are never public.

4.3 **Guardians** of Juniors, as described in the [Guardian and Youth Terms](/legal/guardians-and-youth).

4.4 **Service providers** that run parts of Duna for us under contracts that limit their use of the information: sign-in, payments, hosting, database, file and video storage, video streaming, maps, weather, email, text and push messaging, real-time voice, AI models and workflow tools. They are listed in our [Service Providers list](/legal/subprocessors).

4.5 **Stripe** processes payments and identity verification as an independent business under its own privacy policy (<https://stripe.com/privacy>).

4.6 **Legal and safety:** to comply with law or legal process, protect someone's safety, investigate fraud or misuse, or enforce our terms. Where the law allows, we tell you first.

4.7 **Business transfers:** to a buyer or successor if Duna is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this policy.

4.8 **With your consent** for anything else.

## 5. Public information and imported results

5.1 Public profiles, ratings, rankings, event pages, rosters, results and public videos can be seen by anyone and found by search engines and AI answer services.

5.2 We import results from public sports results sites and tournament organizers. When a result names a player who has no Duna account, we may create an unclaimed profile. Unclaimed profiles are private unless the player competes professionally. You can claim, correct, limit or ask us to remove your profile at support@duna.coach.

## 6. AI features

6.1 Ask Duna, voice onboarding, match summaries, video insights, transcription and image generation send the information needed for each request to AI model providers we use (listed in the [Service Providers list](/legal/subprocessors)). We require them not to use it to train their models.

6.2 We do not send Health data to AI models.

6.3 For Juniors, we do not send messages, voice or video to an AI model unless the provider keeps no copy and, for Children, a Guardian has given verifiable parental consent. AI video review and transcription are adult-only.

6.4 Ask Duna conversations in Duna HQ are kept so organizations can review them, and authorized Duna staff may read them for support and safety.

## 7. Video

Video you upload or stream is stored privately unless you make it public or share a link. Junior-owned videos cannot be made public. People tagged in a video can hide it from their own profile. Video analysis may detect body positions to set up the camera and measure play; we do not use face recognition. See the [Content, Video and Likeness Policy](/legal/content-and-video).

## 9. Security

We use access controls by role, encryption in transit, encryption at rest for sensitive information (including Health data), logging of access to sensitive data, and an incident response process. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## 10. Your choices

- **Profile and visibility:** in Account Settings.
- **Location, camera, microphone, contacts, Health and notifications:** in your device settings and in the app.
- **Messages:** see the [Messaging and Notifications Terms](/legal/messaging). Reply STOP to texts.
- **Cookies and storage:** see the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).
- **Opt-out signals:** we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of today. We honor Global Privacy Control and similar browser signals as a request to opt out of sale and sharing, and will apply them if our practices change.

## 11. Your rights

11.1 Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know what personal information we hold and how we use it, and get a copy in a portable format;
- correct it;
- delete it;
- opt out of sale, sharing for targeted advertising, and profiling with significant effects (we do none of these);
- limit or withdraw consent for sensitive information, including Health data;
- appeal our decision on your request;
- not be treated differently for using these rights.

These rights come from laws in states including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia. We offer the core rights to everyone in the US.

11.2 **How to use them.** Use Account Settings to view, edit, export or delete your information. Or email privacy@duna.coach. [Owner: add a toll-free number if required.] We verify your request by asking you to confirm from your signed-in account or email. An authorized agent must show your written permission, and we may ask you to confirm it.

11.3 **Timing.** We respond within 45 days, and tell you if we need up to another 45 days.

11.4 **Appeals.** If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing privacy@duna.coach with "Appeal" in the subject. We respond within 60 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

11.5 **California.** In the last 12 months we collected the categories in section 2 for the purposes in section 3 and disclosed them for business purposes to the recipients in section 4. Sensitive personal information we collect (account sign-in, precise location, health, information about children, and the contents of messages) is used only as permitted without a right to limit. We do not sell or share personal information, including of consumers under 16.
