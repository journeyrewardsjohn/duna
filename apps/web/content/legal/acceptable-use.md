# Acceptable Use and Community Rules

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the rules below)
>
> - Protect young players. Never contact a junior privately.
> - Treat people with respect. No harassment, hate or threats.
> - Play fair: real results, real names, no rating manipulation.
> - Only post what you have the right to post.
> - Don't misuse or attack the platform.

## 1. Scope

These rules apply to everyone who uses Duna, including Organizations and their Authorized Users. They are part of the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Duna HQ Terms](/legal/hq-terms).

## 2. Safeguarding young players

- Do not contact a Junior privately. Communications with Juniors on Duna always include a Guardian; do not try to move them off Duna to avoid that.
- Do not request, send or share sexual content involving anyone under 18. We report child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement.
- Do not groom, exploit or endanger a Junior, or ask a Junior for personal contact details, photos or meetings outside an Organization's activities.
- Organizations must follow the safe-sport and screening rules that apply to them, including the US Center for SafeSport Code where it applies, and report abuse as the law requires.

## 3. Respect

- No harassment, bullying, stalking, threats, or encouraging self-harm.
- No hate speech or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age or other protected characteristic.
- No sharing someone's private information (such as address, phone or health data) without permission.
- No impersonation of a person, club or Duna.

## 4. Fair results and Sand Rating

- Enter only results that happened, with the players who played.
- Do not play under another person's account, or let someone play under yours.
- Do not arrange results, throw matches, or create fake matches or accounts to change a Sand Rating or ranking.
- Do not misstate your age, division or eligibility.
- Report a wrong result through the app; do not edit results you are not authorized to edit.

## 5. Content standards

- Post only content you own or have permission to use, including music in videos.
- No sexual content, graphic violence, or content that promotes illegal activity.
- No spam, misleading claims or undisclosed advertising.
- No recording people where they expect privacy, such as changing rooms or restrooms.
- No deceptive AI-generated content of real people.

## 6. Platform integrity

- Do not scrape, copy or harvest data from Duna without our written permission, other than through public pages for personal use.
- Do not try to get around security, rate limits, age gates, Guardian controls or payment rules.
- Do not upload malware or interfere with the Service.
- Do not use bots or automation in ways that are not authorized.
- Do not resell access to Duna or use it to run gambling or betting.
- Do not move payments off Duna for Bookings made through Duna in order to avoid fees.

## 7. Reporting

Report a message, profile, video or result in the app, or email support@duna.coach. For safeguarding concerns involving a Junior, we aim to review reports within 24 hours. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

## 8. What we do

We may warn you, remove content, limit features, suspend or close accounts, cancel transactions, preserve evidence, and report to authorities. We act in proportion to the harm and tell you why unless that would create risk. You may appeal to support@duna.coach within 30 days.
