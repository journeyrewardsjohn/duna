# Duna HQ Terms

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the terms below)
>
> - Your organization is our customer. The person who accepts these terms must have authority to bind it, and the organization is responsible for everyone it invites.
> - Plans: Free (5% fee on sales), Club ($199 a month, 2.5%) and Scale ($499 a month, 0%). Paid plans renew until cancelled.
> - You run your business: you set prices, run sessions safely, safeguard minors, honor bookings, and follow Duna's minimum refund standards.
> - Payments run through Duna Pay and Stripe under the separate Duna Pay Terms, including when payouts are released.
> - Your customer data is yours to direct; Duna processes it for you under the data processing addendum below. Player accounts are Duna's.
> - Ask Duna proposes changes; a person on your team approves them.

## 1. The agreement

1.1 These Duna HQ Terms ("**HQ Terms**") are between Beach Elite LLC, doing business as Duna ("**Duna**"), [confirm legal entity], and the business identified when the workspace is created (the "**Organization**", "**you**").

1.2 They cover Duna HQ, Duna Pro, the club website product, the drill library and marketplace, Ask Duna for organizations and related services (the "**HQ Service**").

1.3 The [Duna Pay Terms](/legal/duna-pay) apply when you take payments through Duna. The [Acceptable Use and Community Rules](/legal/acceptable-use), [Messaging and Notifications Terms](/legal/messaging), [Booking and Refund Policy](/legal/booking-and-refunds) and [Copyright and Takedown Policy](/legal/copyright) also apply. Annex A (Data Processing Addendum) is part of these HQ Terms.

1.4 If a signed order form conflicts with these HQ Terms, the order form controls for that purchase.

1.5 Terms defined in the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) have the same meaning here.

## 2. Who binds the Organization

2.1 The person who accepts these HQ Terms confirms that they are at least 18 and have authority to bind the Organization. If they do not, they are personally bound.

2.2 Each Organization is a separate workspace with its own plan, data, payments and responsibilities. One person may belong to several.

2.3 Keep your legal name, address, tax details, payout details and owner contacts accurate.

## 3. Authorized Users

3.1 "**Authorized Users**" are people the Organization invites or permits to use the HQ Service for it, in roles such as owner, director, manager, coach, front desk, scorekeeper and accountant, and any contractor or volunteer you invite.

3.2 You decide who to invite and what role they get. Give each person the least access they need, and remove access promptly when someone leaves.

3.3 **You are responsible for your Authorized Users' actions** in the HQ Service as if they were your own, including their messages to players and handling of personal information.

3.4 Authorized Users must be at least 18. [Owner: confirm; Juniors should not hold staff roles.]

3.5 Each Authorized User also has a personal Duna account under the Duna Terms of Service. Their personal account remains theirs when they leave your Organization; your Organization data does not.

3.6 Tell us promptly at support@duna.coach if you suspect an account has been compromised. We may require multi-factor authentication or extra checks for high-risk actions.

## 4. Plans, billing and renewal

4.1 **Plans.** At the date of this draft:

| Plan  | Monthly | Yearly | Organization Fee on sales through Duna Pay | Video included each month       |
| ----- | ------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------- |
| Free  | $0      | $0     | 5%                                         | 10 hours upload, 2 hours live   |
| Club  | $199    | $1,990 | 2.5%                                       | 100 hours upload, 10 hours live |
| Scale | $499    | $4,990 | 0%                                         | 500 hours upload, 40 hours live |

The Organization Fee and payment processing costs are explained in the [Duna Pay Terms](/legal/duna-pay). Current prices are shown at sign-up and in billing settings. Taxes are extra.

4.2 **Add-ons.** Video packs and pay-as-you-go video, and the club website ($200 a month at the date of this draft), are billed at the prices shown when you add them. On the Free plan, extra video allowance may be earned based on net Organization Fees; refunds and disputes reduce it.

4.3 **Renewal.** Paid plans and add-ons renew automatically for the same period until cancelled. We charge the payment method on file.

4.4 **Changes.** Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current period. We do not prorate. If a paid plan is not active (for example, a failed payment), your sales carry the Free plan's Organization Fee until it is.

4.5 **Cancellation.** Cancel in billing settings. Access to paid features continues to the end of the paid period. Fees are not refunded except where these HQ Terms say otherwise or the law requires.

4.6 **Price changes.** We give at least 30 days' notice of a price or Organization Fee increase. It applies from your next renewal after the notice.

4.7 **Late payment.** If plan fees are unpaid after notice, we may move you to the Free plan or suspend paid features. We may set off unpaid fees against your proceeds as described in the Duna Pay Terms.

## 5. Your obligations

5.1 You will:

- operate lawfully and hold the licences, permits and insurance your activities need;
- describe Offerings accurately, including price, location, time, eligibility, minimum numbers and the Club Policy;
- honor Bookings, run Offerings with appropriate care and staff, and follow the [Booking and Refund Policy](/legal/booking-and-refunds), including the Duna Floor;
- set and show a cancellation and refund policy before purchase;
- present and keep any waiver or release you require, and decide its content and whether it is enforceable;
- safeguard minors: screen staff and volunteers who work with minors as the law and your governing bodies require; follow safe-sport rules (including the US Center for SafeSport Code where it applies); keep Guardians included in communications with Juniors; and never use Duna to contact a Junior privately;
- fulfil goods you sell, state a shipping or pick-up time, and handle returns;
- handle the employment, tax and insurance obligations for your staff and contractors;
- comply with privacy, consumer, accessibility, anti-discrimination, messaging and tax laws that apply to you.

5.2 Duna's tools (safeguarding checks, waivers, messaging rules, reports) help you. They do not replace your duties.

## 6. Duna's role

6.1 Duna provides software and, through Duna Pay, payment services in partnership with Stripe. Duna is not your employer, agent, partner, insurer or the provider of your Offerings.

6.2 For payments, Duna acts as your limited payment collection agent: a player's payment to Duna for your Offering counts as payment to you. Details are in the Duna Pay Terms.

## 7. Data

7.1 **Organization Data.** Information you or your Authorized Users put into the HQ Service about your business, customers, staff, schedules, Offerings, waivers and operations, and information Duna collects on your behalf through your Offerings, is "**Organization Data**". You control it. Duna processes it for you under Annex A.

7.2 **Duna Accounts.** Each player and staff member has a personal Duna account. Duna controls account, sign-in, security, Sand Rating, public profile, Duna Premium, Duna Health and platform-wide data, under the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). When a player books with you, you receive the information you need to provide the Offering. You may not use Duna to export player data for unrelated purposes.

7.3 **Health data.** You may see a player's Duna Health data only under a time-limited grant the player (or, for a Teen, their Guardian) creates. You may use it only to coach or care for that player, not for selection, pricing, eligibility, marketing or employment decisions, and you may not copy it out of Duna. ## 8. Messaging

8.1 You are the sender of messages you or your Authorized Users send to your customers. You must have the consent the law requires before sending texts, especially marketing texts, and honor opt-outs.

8.2 Duna applies platform rules that you cannot switch off: messages with Juniors include a Guardian; messages involving Juniors may be screened before delivery; quiet hours; blocks; and opt-outs.

8.3 Duna may block messages that create legal, deliverability or safety risk. See the [Messaging and Notifications Terms](/legal/messaging).

## 9. AI features

9.1 Ask Duna and other AI features can draft, summarize, recommend and prepare changes in your workspace. **Ask Duna proposes; a person approves.** Changes to your data, prices, schedules or messages take effect only when an Authorized User with the right role approves them. Proposals expire if not approved.

9.2 AI output can be wrong. You are responsible for reviewing it, especially where it affects money, access, staff, minors, safety or legal rights.

9.3 Duna does not use your Organization Data to train AI models that serve other organizations.

9.4 Duna may keep AI conversation records for support and safety. Authorized Duna staff may review them.

10.3 **Domains.** You may use a Duna-provided address or connect your own domain. You control your own domain and its DNS. If Duna registers a domain for you, it is registered in your name or transferred to you on request. [Owner: confirm domain ownership model.]

## 11. Drill library and marketplace

11.1 You may create drills and other training content for your own use, share them, and, where available, sell licences to other organizations.

11.2 **Licence to buyers.** A buyer receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the drill within its own organization, unless the listing says otherwise. Buyers may not resell or republish drills.

11.3 **Sellers.** You confirm you own or have the rights to what you list. Duna may review listings before they appear and may refuse or remove them. Sales are processed through Duna Pay.

11.4 Duna has a licence to host, display and deliver listed drills to buyers.

12.1 Team scheduling, availability, compensation settings, earnings goals and pay tracking are records for your administration. **Duna is not the employer of your staff and is not a payroll provider.**

12.2 You decide whether each person is an employee or an independent contractor, and you are responsible for wages, withholding, benefits, tax forms and insurance.

12.3 If Duna Pay offers contractor payouts, those are payments you instruct, from your funds, to recipients you choose. Duna does not decide pay. [Not available today.]

The [Acceptable Use and Community Rules](/legal/acceptable-use) apply.

15.2 We give notice and a chance to fix the problem first, unless the risk is urgent. We restore access when the issue is resolved.

15.3 Held funds during a suspension are handled under the Duna Pay Terms.

## Annex A: Data Processing Addendum

> Roles in this Annex are drafted for counsel to confirm (counsel question C7).

A.1 **Roles.** For Organization Data, the Organization is the controller (or "business") and Duna is its processor (or "service provider"). For Duna account data described in section 7.2, Duna is an independent controller. Where both have a role in the same data (for example, a Booking), each is responsible for its own processing.

A.2 **Instructions.** Duna processes Organization Data only to provide the HQ Service, on the Organization's documented instructions (these HQ Terms, settings and support requests), and as the law requires. Duna tells the Organization if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

A.3 **Service-provider terms.** Duna will not sell or share Organization Data for cross-context behavioural advertising, retain, use or disclose it outside the direct business relationship, or combine it with other data except as the law permits for a service provider.

A.4 **Personnel.** People at Duna who access Organization Data are bound by confidentiality.

A.5 **Security.** Duna maintains administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the data, including access controls by role, encryption in transit, encryption at rest for sensitive categories, logging of access to sensitive data, and an incident response process.

A.6 **Subprocessors.** The Organization authorizes Duna to use the subprocessors in the [Service Providers list](/legal/subprocessors). Duna gives at least 30 days' notice of a new subprocessor by updating the list and notifying account owners who subscribe. The Organization may object on reasonable data-protection grounds; if we cannot resolve it, the Organization may terminate the affected service with a pro-rata refund. Duna's contracts with subprocessors contain data-protection terms at least as protective as this Annex.

A.7 **Assistance.** Duna helps the Organization respond to individuals' privacy requests and with assessments that the law requires, taking into account the information available to Duna.

A.8 **Incidents.** Duna notifies the Organization without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after confirming a security incident affecting Organization Data, with the information reasonably available. [Owner/counsel: confirm 72 hours.]

A.9 **Deletion and return.** On termination, Duna makes Organization Data available for export for 30 days, then deletes it within a further 60 days, except copies kept in backups (which expire on their schedule) and records the law requires Duna to keep.

A.10 **Audits.** On reasonable request, no more than once a year, Duna provides information needed to show compliance with this Annex.

A.11 **Location.** Duna processes Organization Data in the United States.

A.12 **Children.** Where Organization Data includes information about Children, the Organization confirms it has obtained any parental consent required for its own collection, and Duna applies the [Children's Privacy Notice](/legal/childrens-privacy) to its processing.
