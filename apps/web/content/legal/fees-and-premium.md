# Player Fees and Duna Premium

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the terms below)
>
> - Some bookings carry a Duna service fee. You always see it, and the total, before you pay.
> - If the club cancels or does not deliver, you get the service fee back. If you cancel yourself, you get the booking price back under the club's policy, but not the service fee.
> - Duna Premium renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel online in a few taps; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.
> - Credits work only at the club that issued them, are not cash, and paid credits do not expire.

## 1. Scope

This document is part of the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and uses its definitions.

## 2. The Service Fee

2.1 **What it is.** A fee Duna charges the buyer for using Duna to find, book and pay. It is Duna's fee, not the Organization's.

2.2 **When it applies.** On card payments for Bookings, event registrations and tickets, Organization memberships and packages. It does not apply to physical goods, payments made with Credits, cash payments, in-person card payments taken by an Organization, or loading a wallet.

2.3 **How much.** 7.5% of the price of the items it applies to, before tax. [Owner: confirm the rate; it is set in code at 7.5%.] For recurring memberships it is charged again with each renewal.

2.4 **Shown before you pay.** We show the Service Fee and the total before you confirm payment. Where state law requires prices to include mandatory fees from the first time a price is shown (for example, California), the price you see already includes the Service Fee. [Engineering gap: all-in prices are not shown today.]

2.5 **Duna Premium.** Duna Premium members pay no Service Fee.

2.6 **When it is refunded.**

- The Organization cancels, does not deliver, or materially changes the Booking: refunded in full.
- Duna made an error, or the charge was a duplicate or unauthorized: refunded in full.
- You cancel within the Organization's policy, or the Organization agrees to a refund as a courtesy: the Booking price is refunded; the Service Fee is not.
- A chargeback or law requires otherwise: as required.

[Owner decision; the code today refunds the Service Fee on every refund. California and other all-in pricing laws may limit a non-refundable fee: counsel question.]

## 3. Duna Premium

3.1 **Plans.** Duna Premium is offered as Premium and Premium+, billed monthly or yearly. Current prices are shown on the plan page and at checkout. At the date of this draft they are:

| Plan     | Monthly | Yearly |
| -------- | ------- | ------ |
| Premium  | $9.99   | $99    |
| Premium+ | $29.99  | $299   |

Tax is added where it applies.

3.2 **What it includes.** The features listed on the plan page when you subscribe. At the date of this draft:

- no Service Fee on eligible Bookings;
- more video storage and live streaming each month (Premium: 8 hours upload, 2 hours live; Premium+: 30 hours upload, 8 hours live);
- live broadcasting;
- full Sand Rating history and partner insights;
- comments on matches (with a verified account);
- 1,000 prediction credits a month instead of 100;
- Premium+ also includes advanced video insights and priority video processing.

We may improve features. If we remove a main feature during your billing period, you may cancel and receive a pro-rata refund for the rest of that period.

3.3 **Who can subscribe.** Adults only.

3.4 **Free trials.** There is no free trial today. If we offer one, we tell you before you start: how long it lasts, the price after it ends, and how to cancel. We remind you before a trial longer than one month converts.

3.5 **Automatic renewal.** **Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each monthly or yearly period, and we charge your payment method the then-current price, until you cancel.** At checkout we show the price, the billing period, that it renews automatically, and how to cancel, next to the button you press to agree, and we send a confirmation by email that you can keep.

3.6 **How to cancel.** Cancel online at any time from Account Settings > Duna Premium on duna.coach, or from your profile in the Duna app. It takes no more steps than subscribing. You can also email support@duna.coach. We confirm the cancellation by email.

3.7 **Effect of cancelling.** Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then stops. We do not refund the unused part of a period, except where section 3.2, section 3.10 or the law requires.

3.8 **Pause.** You can pause Premium one month at a time, for up to four months in total. You are not charged, and Premium features are off, while paused.

3.9 **Reminders and price changes.** For yearly plans we send a reminder 15 to 45 days before renewal with the price and how to cancel. We give you at least 30 days' notice of a price increase, and the new price applies from your next renewal after the notice. You can cancel before it applies. New York residents who are charged a higher price without having agreed to it may cancel within 14 days after the charge and receive a pro-rata refund. Other refunds are at our discretion or as the law requires.

3.11 **Where you can buy it.** Duna Premium is sold on duna.coach. You can use it in the Duna apps once you subscribe.

> **Note for the owner (not part of the published terms): Apple's rules for Duna Premium in the iOS app.** Duna Premium unlocks digital features inside the app (video hours, live broadcasting, insights, comments, prediction credits). Apple App Review Guideline 3.1.1 requires in-app purchase to unlock digital features, and 3.1.3(b) lets an app unlock a subscription bought elsewhere only if the same subscription is also offered by in-app purchase. In the US storefront Apple now allows a button or link to a web checkout, and, while the Epic v. Apple case is pending in the Supreme Court (argument set for January 2027), no commission is currently collectible on link-out purchases. So: (1) Bookings, lessons, events, goods and other services used outside the app must **not** use in-app purchase (Guideline 3.1.3(e)); (2) Duna Premium should be offered by in-app purchase in the iOS app, optionally alongside a US link to web checkout; (3) the Service Fee waiver is a benefit on real-world services, but it is bundled with digital features, so it does not take Premium outside 3.1.1. Selling Premium only on the web while the app unlocks it, with no in-app purchase, is likely to be rejected. If in-app purchase is added, these terms need an Apple-billing section (Apple handles cancellation and refunds for those subscriptions). Counsel question C9.

## 4. Credits and credit packs

4.1 **What a Credit is.** A unit an Organization issues that you can use for that Organization's Offerings. Credits are issued and honored by the Organization, not Duna.

4.2 **Not cash.** Credits have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, and can be used only with the issuing Organization. They are not a bank account, deposit or gift card issued by Duna.

4.3 **Expiry.** Credits you buy in a paid credit pack do not expire. Credits included with a membership expire at the end of that membership period. Promotional Credits an Organization gives you may expire on the date shown when they are given. When you use Credits, the ones expiring soonest are used first.

4.4 **Members-only packs.** An Organization may sell some packs only to its members or participants.

4.5 **Transfers.** Credits cannot be transferred, except that a Guardian may move Credits to a Junior they manage at the same Organization.

4.6 **Refunds.** An unused credit pack can be refunded in full to the original payment method. A partly used pack is refunded under the Organization's policy, and at least for the unused Credits at the price per Credit you paid. [Engineering gap: partly used packs cannot be refunded today.] Purchases paid with Credits are refunded in Credits.

4.7 **If an Organization closes.** The Organization must honor or refund unused paid Credits. Duna holds back part of the Organization's funds to help cover these refunds and will help you claim one. See the [Duna Pay Terms](/legal/duna-pay) section 7.

## 5. Promo and gift codes

Promo codes are issued by Duna or an Organization, have the terms shown with them, have no cash value unless stated, and may not be sold. Duna does not sell gift cards today.

## 6. Taxes

Prices exclude sales tax unless stated. We calculate and show tax at checkout. Duna may collect and remit sales tax as a marketplace facilitator or as the seller for tax purposes, where the law requires.

## 7. Failed payments

If a recurring payment fails, we or Stripe may retry it over the following days and tell you. If it still fails, the subscription or membership may be paused or ended, and access stops. You remain responsible for amounts owed for periods already provided.
