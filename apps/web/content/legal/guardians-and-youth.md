# Guardian and Youth Terms

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the terms below)
>
> - A parent or legal guardian manages every player under 18 on Duna.
> - Children under 13 never have their own account. Teens aged 13 to 17 have their own sign-in, linked to a guardian.
> - Guardians pay for and approve bookings, sign waivers, and are copied on every message between a junior and a coach or club.
> - Junior profiles and videos are never public.
> - Duna Health is not available to anyone under 13. Teens can use it only with a guardian's approval, once that option is available.
> - You can see, change, export or delete your child's information, and withdraw consent, at any time.

## 1. Who these terms are for

1.1 These terms add to the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and use the same definitions. They apply to every Guardian and every Junior on Duna.

1.2 By linking a Junior to your account, you confirm that you are that Junior's parent or legal guardian, or are otherwise legally authorized to act for them, and that you accept these terms and the Terms of Service for yourself and on the Junior's behalf.

## 2. How a Guardian is linked

2.1 A Guardian can add a Junior as a dependant from their own account, or accept an invitation sent by a Teen.

2.2 Duna reviews new Guardian links before they are treated as verified. Until then, some features stay off. We may ask for more information. [Engineering note: today, review is by Duna staff; see `docs/legal/README.md`.]

2.3 A Junior may have more than one Guardian. Each Guardian can manage the Junior. If Guardians disagree about consent, the more protective choice applies until they agree.

2.4 If you no longer have authority for a Junior (for example, because of a court order), tell us at support@duna.coach and unlink them.

## 4. Bookings, payments and waivers

4.1 Guardians make and pay for Bookings for Juniors. Charges go to the Guardian's payment method. The Guardian is responsible for them.

4.2 Credits a Guardian buys may be moved to a Junior's account with the same Organization. Credits keep their original expiry.

4.3 Organizations may require a waiver or release for a Junior. Only a verified Guardian can sign it. A Teen may also be asked to acknowledge it, but the Teen's acknowledgment alone is never enough. The waiver is between the Guardian, the Junior and the Organization. Whether a waiver signed by a parent is enforceable depends on state law.

4.4 Duna never makes payment depend on signing a waiver, but an Organization may refuse participation until it is signed.

## 5. Messages

5.1 Every conversation between a Junior and a coach, club or Organization includes a verified Guardian. Coaches cannot message a Junior privately.

5.2 Messages involving Juniors may be held for a safety check before delivery. Attachments sent to or by Juniors are reviewed by a person.

5.3 Texts and email go to the Guardian's phone and email, not the Junior's, unless the Guardian adds the Teen's number and the Teen agrees.

5.4 Anyone can block a sender. A block overrides every relationship.

5.5 Report a concern about a message in the app or at support@duna.coach. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911.

## 6. Photos and video of Juniors

6.1 Videos a Junior owns are private or link-only. They cannot be made public.

6.2 Only a Guardian, or the Organization under its own media consent from the Guardian, may upload video in which a Junior is the subject. Others must not upload or share video of a Junior without the Guardian's permission.

6.3 A Guardian may ask us to remove video showing their Junior from any Duna surface we control. We act on these requests promptly, and within 7 days at the latest. [Owner: confirm 7 days.]

6.4 Junior video is never used to train machine-learning models, and AI video review is not available for Juniors.

6.5 See the [Content, Video and Likeness Policy](/legal/content-and-video).

## 7. Duna Health for Juniors

7.1 Duna Health reads fitness data from Apple Health. It is available only to people using a US App Store account.

7.2 **Under 13:** not available. It stays off until Duna offers verifiable parental consent for it, and these terms are updated.

7.3 **13 to 17:** available only when both the Teen opts in on their own device and a verified Guardian approves. Until that approval flow is available in your app, Duna Health is limited to adults. When it is available:

- only a limited set of metrics can be connected: heart rate, workouts (including heart-rate zones), active energy and exercise minutes;
- sharing is possible only with a coach or the Organization, never with other players, for no more than 90 days at a time, and each share needs the Guardian's approval;
- data is kept for no more than 12 months;
- the Guardian sees the connection status, the metrics enabled and the shares, and can pause, revoke, disconnect and delete. A Guardian does not see a Teen's health values unless the Teen agrees.

7.4 At 18, Guardian-approved shares end and the player is asked to consent again.

7.5 See the [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/consumer-health-privacy).

## 8. AI features and Juniors

8.1 AI features that would send a Junior's messages, voice or video to an outside AI model are off for Juniors, unless the model provider has agreed not to keep the data and, for Children, a Guardian has given verifiable parental consent.

8.2 Voice onboarding is for adults. [Engineering gap: no age check on voice onboarding today.]

8.3 Duna never uses a Junior's data to train AI models.

## 9. Your rights over your Junior's information

9.1 From your Guardian account you can review your Junior's profile, Bookings, Credits, messages that include you, waivers, Health connection status and settings.

9.2 You can correct information, export it, withdraw a consent, or ask us to delete the Junior's account. Deletion follows the same 7-day recovery window as other accounts.

9.3 You can also email privacy@duna.coach. We respond within 45 days. For Children, we will not ask for more information than we need to confirm you are the Guardian.

9.4 Organizations keep their own records of a Junior (such as attendance and waivers). Ask the Organization for those.

## 10. How consent is given and withdrawn

10.1 You give consent through a specific screen that explains what you are agreeing to. We record who consented, when, the version of the text, and how.

10.2 For Children, any feature that collects information directly from the Child needs verifiable parental consent in a method COPPA accepts. [Owner: method to be chosen; see `docs/legal/README.md`.]

10.3 You can withdraw a consent at any time in settings or at privacy@duna.coach. Withdrawal stops future collection or sharing for that purpose. It does not undo actions already taken, such as a completed Booking.

## 11. Birthdays

At 13, a Child may get their own sign-in, and we ask the new Teen to agree to the Terms with the Guardian. At 18, Guardian controls end, and we ask the new adult to review their settings, consents and privacy choices.
