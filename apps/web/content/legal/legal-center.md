# Duna Legal Center

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version**
>
> Here you can find every agreement and policy for Duna. Each one starts with a plain-language summary. The summaries help you find your way; the full text is what applies.

## For players and families

**[Terms of Service](/legal/terms).** The main agreement for anyone who uses Duna to play, book, follow or manage a family. It explains accounts and ages, Duna's role as a platform, bookings, Sand Rating, your content, AI features, risk, liability and how disputes are resolved.

**[Guardian and Youth Terms](/legal/guardians-and-youth).** What parents and guardians agree to, what players under 18 can do at each age, and how messages, video, health data and consent work for juniors.

**[Player Fees and Duna Premium](/legal/fees-and-premium).** The Duna service fee and when it is refunded, Duna Premium and how to cancel it, credits, promo codes, taxes and failed payments.

**[Booking and Refund Policy](/legal/booking-and-refunds).** How club policies and Duna's minimum standards work together for cancellations, weather, no-shows, waitlists, transfers and refunds.

**[Mobile App License](/legal/eula).** The licence for the Duna and Duna Pro apps.

## For clubs and organizations

**[Duna HQ Terms](/legal/hq-terms).** The agreement for organizations and everyone they invite: plans, users, your obligations, data and the data processing addendum, messaging, AI, club websites, drills, team tools and liability.

**[Duna Pay Terms](/legal/duna-pay).** How payments, fees, releases and payouts, holdbacks, refunds, disputes and negative balances work, with Stripe.

## Privacy

**[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).** What we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights.

**[Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/consumer-health-privacy).** How Duna Health data from Apple Health is collected, shared, kept and deleted.

**[Children's Privacy Notice](/legal/childrens-privacy).** How Duna handles information about children under 13 and parents' rights.

**[Cookie Policy](/legal/cookies).** The cookies and browser storage Duna uses.

**[Service Providers](/legal/subprocessors).** The companies that process personal information for Duna.

## Policies

**[Acceptable Use and Community Rules](/legal/acceptable-use).** Safeguarding, respect, fair results and ratings, content and platform rules.

**[Messaging and Notifications Terms](/legal/messaging).** Channels, consent to texts, opting out, and guardians in conversations with juniors.

**[Content, Video and Likeness Policy](/legal/content-and-video).** Recording, sharing and removing video, juniors on camera, likeness and music.

**[Copyright and Takedown Policy](/legal/copyright).** How to send a copyright notice or counter-notice.

## What changed in version 1.0

- New documents: Guardian and Youth Terms, Player Fees and Duna Premium, Booking and Refund Policy, Duna Pay Terms, Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, Children's Privacy Notice, Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use and Community Rules, Messaging and Notifications Terms, Content, Video and Likeness Policy, Copyright and Takedown Policy, and Service Providers.
- Terms of Service: rewritten around Duna as a platform, with age bands, the Duna minimum refund standards, AI features and a dispute section.
- Duna HQ Terms: rewritten, with plans, authorized users, club websites, drills, team tools and a data processing addendum. Payments moved to the Duna Pay Terms.
- Duna Pay Terms: release of funds on delivery, payouts within 3 business days, and clear rules for disputes, refunds, reserves and negative balances.
- Privacy Policy: rewritten to match what Duna collects today; no analytics or advertising; state privacy rights and opt-out signals.
