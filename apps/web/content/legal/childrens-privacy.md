# Children's Privacy Notice

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the notice below)
>
> - A child under 13 never has their own Duna account. A parent or guardian manages them.
> - Features that would collect information directly from a child stay off until a parent gives verifiable consent.
> - Duna Health is not available for children.
> - You can review, change or delete your child's information, and stop further collection, at any time.

## 1. Who this applies to

This notice explains how Beach Elite LLC, doing business as Duna, [confirm legal entity], [Duna mailing address], privacy@duna.coach, handles personal information about children under 13 ("**Children**"), as the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires. It adds to the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 2. How a Child comes to be on Duna

2.1 A parent or legal guardian ("**Guardian**") adds the Child as a dependant from the Guardian's own account. The Child does not sign in.

2.2 If someone signing up gives a birth date under 13, we stop the sign-up and ask for a Guardian, and delete what was entered within 30 days if no Guardian completes it. [Engineering gap: not built.]

2.3 An Organization may record a Child it coaches (for example, on a roster or attendance list) as part of its own records, under its own consent from the Guardian.

## 3. What we collect about a Child

From the Guardian or Organization: name, birth date, the Guardian link, Bookings, waivers the Guardian signs, emergency contact flag, attendance, match results, Sand Rating, and photos or video the Guardian or Organization uploads.

We do not collect information directly from a Child today. Features that would (such as the Child's own sign-in, messaging, voice or video features used by the Child, or Duna Health) stay off for Children until the Guardian has given verifiable parental consent for them.

## 4. How we use it

To manage the Child's Bookings and participation, calculate private results and Sand Rating, keep the Child safe (for example, keeping the Guardian in every conversation with a coach), and to comply with law. We do not use it for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models.

## 5. Who we share it with

- The Organizations the Guardian books with, as needed for the Booking.
- Service providers that run Duna for us, under contracts limiting their use. See the [Service Providers list](/legal/subprocessors).
- As the law requires.

A Child's profile, results and videos are never public. We do not share a Child's information with other third parties. We ask for a separate consent before any disclosure that is not part of providing the Service.

## 6. Verifiable parental consent

When a Guardian turns on a feature that collects information from a Child, we first give the Guardian a direct notice of what we collect, how we use and share it, and how long we keep it, and ask for consent by a method COPPA accepts. [Owner: choose the method, for example a card verification through Stripe with a small charge or authorization. See `docs/legal/README.md`.]

## 7. Parents' rights

A Guardian can, at any time:

- review the information we hold about the Child;
- correct it;
- refuse further collection or use, and withdraw consent;
- ask us to delete it.

Use the Guardian account, or email privacy@duna.coach. We verify that you are the Guardian before acting.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep a Child's information while the Guardian keeps the dependant on Duna. When a Guardian removes the Child or deletes their account, we delete the Child's information after a 7-day recovery window, except payment, waiver and consent records the law requires us to keep (up to 7 years), which we restrict. Backups clear within 6 months. If a dependant has had no activity for 24 months, we ask the Guardian whether to keep it and delete it if we do not hear back within 30 days. ## 9. Security

We keep a written information security program, with a named person responsible, that covers Children's information. Children's information is protected by the safeguards in the Privacy Policy.
