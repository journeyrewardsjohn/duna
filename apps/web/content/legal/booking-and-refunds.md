# Booking and Refund Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - Each club sets its own cancellation policy. You see it before you pay.
> - Every club must meet Duna's minimum standards: if the club cancels, doesn't deliver, or makes a big change, you get a full refund, including Duna's service fee.
> - If you cancel within the club's policy, you get the booking price back. Duna's service fee is not refunded.
> - Refunds go back to how you paid, usually within 5 to 10 business days of being approved.

## 1. How this policy works

1.1 This policy is part of the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and uses its definitions.

1.2 Each Organization sets a cancellation and refund policy for its Offerings ("**Club Policy**"). The Club Policy is shown on the Offering page and at checkout. The Club Policy that applies is the one shown when you booked.

1.3 Every Club Policy must meet Duna's minimum standards in section 2 ("**Duna Floor**"). If a Club Policy conflicts with the Duna Floor, the Duna Floor applies.

1.4 An Organization may always be more generous than its Club Policy.

## 2. The Duna Floor

2.1 **The Organization cancels or does not deliver.** You receive a full refund to your original payment method, including the Service Fee. You may choose Credits instead, but the Organization cannot make you.

2.2 **Material change.** If the Organization changes the date, start time by more than 60 minutes [Owner: confirm 60 minutes], venue, format or coach named as a main feature, you may cancel for a full refund, including the Service Fee.

2.3 **Minimum numbers.** If a session needs a minimum number of players and does not reach it, and the Organization cancels it, you receive a full refund. Duna refunds these automatically.

2.4 **Clear policy before you pay.** The Club Policy, including any non-refundable terms, must be shown before you pay.

2.5 **Goods not sent.** If physical goods are not handed over or shipped by the stated date, or within 30 days if no date is given, you may cancel for a full refund.

2.6 **Timely refunds.** Approved refunds are started within 5 business days.

## 4. Weather, darkness and outdoor courts

4.1 Many Offerings are outdoors. The Organization decides whether to play, delay or cancel because of weather, heat, lightning, air quality or darkness. Lightning means stopping play.

4.2 If the Organization cancels before the Offering starts, section 2.1 applies.

4.3 If play stops after it has started, the Club Policy for interrupted play applies (for example, a credit for the unused time). If there is no such policy and less than half the scheduled time was delivered, you are entitled to a refund for the time not delivered.

4.4 If you decide not to play in conditions the Organization considers playable, the Club Policy for cancellations applies.

## 5. No-shows and late cancellation

5.1 If you do not attend, or cancel after the Club Policy's free cancellation time, the Booking is usually not refunded.

5.2 An Organization may charge a no-show fee only if the amount was shown before you booked.

## 6. Waitlists, transfers and substitutions

6.1 Joining a waitlist does not guarantee a place. If a place opens, we tell you. You may need to pay within a stated time or the place goes to the next person. If you were charged when you joined a waitlist and do not get a place, you receive a full refund.

6.2 You may transfer a Booking to another player only if the Offering allows it. The new player must meet the eligibility rules and complete any waiver.

6.3 Team events may allow roster substitutions until a stated deadline.

## 7. Memberships

7.1 Organization memberships renew automatically unless the membership says it ends after a fixed term. The renewal terms, any minimum commitment, and how to cancel are shown at checkout.

7.2 You can cancel online from your Duna account. Depending on the membership, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, or straight away with a full refund within a set number of days, or with a pro-rata refund.

7.3 During a minimum commitment, cancellation takes effect at the end of the commitment.

7.4 We send renewal reminders where the membership says so, and where the law requires.

## 8. How refunds are paid

8.1 Refunds go to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear 5 to 10 business days after they are started, depending on your bank.

8.2 If you paid with Credits, you get Credits back.

8.3 A partial refund is applied to the items refunded, with the Service Fee handled as in section 2 and [Player Fees and Duna Premium](/legal/fees-and-premium) section 2.6.

## 9. Chargebacks

Please contact the Organization or us before asking your bank to reverse a charge. We can usually resolve a problem faster. If you file a chargeback, the Booking may be suspended while it is reviewed. Filing chargebacks for Offerings you received may lead to the account being suspended.

## 10. Asking for help

10.1 Ask for a cancellation or refund from the Booking in your Duna account, or contact the Organization through Duna.

10.2 If the Organization does not respond within 5 business days, or you think the Duna Floor has not been met, email support@duna.coach with your Booking details. We review it and may refund you directly where the Duna Floor applies.
