# Content, Video and Likeness Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - Your videos are private unless you choose otherwise. Juniors' videos are never public.
> - Respect the people you film and the club's rules. Get permission before filming juniors who are not yours.
> - People in your video can hide it from their own profile, and can ask us to remove it.
> - Don't add music you don't have the rights to.

## 1. Scope

This policy is part of the [Duna Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and the [Duna HQ Terms](/legal/hq-terms).

## 2. Recording at clubs and events

2.1 Follow the Organization's rules on recording and streaming at its venue and events. An Organization may forbid recording, or require its own media consent.

2.2 Beach courts are often public places, but people still expect respect. Do not film people in a way that harasses them, and never in changing areas or restrooms.

2.3 Do not record or stream a Junior who is not yours without the Guardian's permission, unless the Organization has that permission and has authorized the recording.

2.4 Organizations that record sessions or stream events must tell participants and, for Juniors, have the Guardian's consent.

## 3. Uploading and visibility

3.1 Live streams are public or link-only. Recordings and uploads are public or private; practice uploads start private.

3.2 Videos owned by a Junior can only be private or link-only.

3.3 Link-only means anyone with the link can watch. We log when share links are used. You can turn a link off.

## 4. Sharing with coaches and clubs

You can share a video with a coach or Organization. They may view it, comment and create clips for coaching. They must not publish it without your permission (and the Guardian's for a Junior).

## 5. People in your videos

5.1 If you are tagged in a video, you can choose whether it appears on your profile.

5.2 If you appear in a video and want it removed from Duna, ask the owner or email support@duna.coach. We remove video of a Junior on a Guardian's request. For adults, we weigh the request against the owner's interest and remove video that breaks these rules or the [Acceptable Use and Community Rules](/legal/acceptable-use).

## 6. Analysis and AI

6.1 Duna may analyse video to detect body positions for camera set-up and to measure play. We do not use face recognition.

6.2 AI video review is available only for adults. Using your video to train models needs your separate consent. Junior video is never used for training.

6.3 With your permission, heart rate from Duna Health may be shown alongside your video. It is visible only to you and to people you share Health data with.

## 7. Likeness and generated images

7.1 Duna can create images or video of you from reference photos you provide. Provide only photos of yourself, or of a Junior you are the Guardian of. You can delete generated images at any time.

7.2 Do not use Duna to create images of a real person without their permission.

7.3 Organizations may use your name and photo for their own Offerings only as their own consent terms allow.

## 8. Music

Do not add music or other content you do not have the rights to. We may mute or remove video after a valid copyright notice. See the [Copyright and Takedown Policy](/legal/copyright).

## 9. Deleting video

You can delete your videos at any time. Deleted videos are removed from Duna and our video providers, and leave backups within 6 months. Copies others downloaded or recorded are outside our control.

## 10. Livestreams to other platforms

If you connect a third-party channel to simulcast, that platform's terms apply to the stream there. You can disconnect it at any time.
