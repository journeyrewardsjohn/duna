# Cookie Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - Duna uses only the cookies and storage it needs to sign you in, keep you secure and remember your settings.
> - We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies today.
> - Some pages show a map or a video, and the provider of that map or video may set its own cookies.

## 1. What this covers

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Websites can also use "local storage" and "session storage" in your browser. This policy covers both, on duna.coach and hq.duna.coach. It is part of the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 2. What Duna sets

**Strictly necessary cookies.**

| Name                        | Purpose                                         | How long                               |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| `wos-session`               | Keeps you signed in, securely (encrypted)       | Until you sign out or the session ends |
| `duna-organization-context` | Remembers which organization you are working in | 90 days                                |

**Preferences and convenience (stored in your browser, not sent to us automatically).**

| Name                                                     | Purpose                                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `duna-theme`                                             | Your light or dark theme choice                                             |
| `duna-ai-recent-player-paths`, `duna-ai-recent-hq-paths` | Recently used Ask Duna destinations                                         |
| `duna-action-center-paths-*`, `duna-search-recents-*`    | Recent searches and shortcuts                                               |
| `duna-scoring-device-id`                                 | Identifies this device when you keep score, so scores are not counted twice |
| `duna-score-pending:*`                                   | Scores saved while you were offline, until they are sent                    |
| `ask-duna:draft:*` (session storage)                     | An unsent Ask Duna message, until you close the tab                         |

## 3. Third parties on our pages

Some pages include content from other providers, which may set their own cookies or collect device information when the content loads:

- **Maps** on event and venue pages.
- **Video players** for match videos and live streams, which may collect playback quality data.
- **Payments**: when you check out, you go to a Stripe-hosted page, which uses its own cookies for security and fraud prevention.

These providers are listed in our [Service Providers list](/legal/subprocessors). Their own policies apply.

## 4. What we do not use

No analytics, advertising, social media or cross-site tracking cookies, and no tag managers or tracking pixels.

## 5. If this changes

If we add analytics or any non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask for your consent before setting them and offer a way to change your choice at any time. We will honor Global Privacy Control signals.

## 6. How to control cookies and storage

You can delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you cannot sign in. Clearing site data resets your preferences and removes unsent offline scores.

## 7. Club websites

Club websites hosted by Duna do not run scripts and do not set analytics or advertising cookies. A club is responsible for anything it adds through its own domain or services.
