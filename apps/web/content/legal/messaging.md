# Messaging and Notifications Terms

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the terms below)
>
> - We message you about your account, bookings and conversations you are in.
> - Texts need your consent. Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help. Message and data rates may apply.
> - Messages with coaches and clubs always include a guardian if the player is under 18.
> - We send no marketing without your consent.

## 1. Channels

Duna may contact you in the app, by push notification, by email and by text message (SMS). For adults in one-to-one conversations with an Organization, messages may also be delivered through iMessage if you link your phone number. Duna does not use WhatsApp or RCS today.

## 2. Types of message

- **Service messages:** sign-in, security, receipts, Booking confirmations and changes, cancellations, waitlist offers, reminders, Guardian approvals, and replies in conversations you are part of. You cannot opt out of essential service messages by email or in the app while you have an account, but you can opt out of service texts (section 4).
- **Organization messages:** messages an Organization sends you about its Offerings. The Organization is the sender and must have your consent where the law requires.
- **Marketing:** promotions from Duna or an Organization. Sent only with your consent, and you can opt out at any time.

## 3. Consent to texts

3.1 When you give your mobile number and agree to receive texts, you consent to receive recurring automated text messages from Duna ([SMS program name]) and from Organizations you deal with through Duna, about your account, Bookings and conversations, at that number. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

3.2 Marketing texts are sent only if you separately agree to them in writing (for example, by checking a box that says so).

3.3 **Frequency:** varies with your activity. **Message and data rates may apply.**

3.4 If someone invites you by text (for example, to a match), the invitation is a single message. You will not receive more texts unless you join and agree.

## 4. Opting out and help

4.1 Reply **STOP** (or QUIT, END, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE) to any text to stop texts of that type from that number. We confirm by one final text. Reply **HELP** for help, or email support@duna.coach.

4.2 Stopping service texts does not stop essential messages by email or in the app.

4.3 You can also manage messages in Account Settings and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in each email.

4.4 We honor opt-outs within 10 business days, and usually immediately.

4.5 Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## 5. Juniors

5.1 Every conversation between a Junior and a coach, club or Organization includes a verified Guardian.

5.2 Texts and emails about a Junior go to the Guardian.

5.3 Messages involving Juniors may be held for a safety check, and attachments are reviewed by a person.

5.4 iMessage delivery is not available for Juniors.

## 6. Quiet hours and blocks

Non-urgent messages from Organizations are not delivered during quiet hours at your local time [Owner: state the hours, for example 9 pm to 8 am]. You can block anyone; a block overrides every relationship.

## 7. Direct messages between players

Players can message each other directly only when they follow each other. Duna is not a marketing channel.

## 8. Privacy

Messages are handled under the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). Phone numbers are never sold or shared for others' marketing.
