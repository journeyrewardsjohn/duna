# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

> **Draft for attorney review. Not in effect.**

> **The short version** (this summary does not replace the policy below)
>
> - Duna Health is optional, read-only, US only, and for fitness and performance, not medical use.
> - We collect health data only after you consent, and share it only with a coach or club you choose, for a limited time, after a separate consent.
> - We never sell it, use it for advertising, or send it to AI models.
> - We keep it for 24 months (adults) or 12 months (teens), and you can delete it at any time.
> - Children under 13 cannot use Duna Health.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy explains how Beach Elite LLC, doing business as Duna, [confirm legal entity], handles "consumer health data" as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and Nevada law (NRS 603A, as amended by SB 370). It applies to everyone who uses Duna Health, wherever they live in the US. It adds to the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

1.2 This policy is linked from the Duna home page.

## 3. The health data we collect

3.1 **From Apple Health, only the categories you choose:**

- heart: heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, walking heart rate, VO2 max;
- recovery: respiratory rate, blood oxygen, body temperature, sleep;
- activity: active energy, steps, distance, exercise minutes, stand minutes, workouts;
- body: weight, body fat percentage, lean body mass.

3.2 **From you:** daily check-ins you choose to complete (for example, how you feel).

3.3 **Derived:** readiness and strain scores, and heart rate shown alongside your match video.

3.4 **For teens:** only heart rate, workouts (including heart-rate zones), active energy and exercise minutes. No check-ins. Readiness and strain stay visible only to the teen.

3.5 We do not write anything to Apple Health. We do not collect precise location in connection with Duna Health, and we do not set up geofences around health care facilities.

## 4. Why we collect it

To show you your own training and recovery trends; to add heart rate to your match videos if you choose; and to share it, if you choose, with a coach or club for a limited time so they can coach you.

## 5. Consent

5.1 **To collect.** We ask for your consent on a dedicated screen before collecting, naming the categories, purposes and how to withdraw. Apple then asks for its own permission. For a teen, the Guardian's approval is also required.

5.2 **To share.** Each time you share with a coach or club, you give a separate consent naming the recipient, the categories, what they can see, and when it ends. For a teen, the Guardian must approve each share.

5.3 **Withdrawing.** You can revoke a share, or disconnect Duna Health, at any time in the app. Revoking stops access straight away.

## 6. Who we share it with

6.1 **Coaches and clubs you choose,** under a share that lasts up to one year (90 days by default); for teens, up to 90 days, and never with other players. We check the coaching or club relationship every time they view it, and log each view.

6.2 **Other players** only if you are an adult and you create a share for them.

6.3 **Our service providers** that store and process data for us (database and hosting), under contracts that forbid any other use.

6.4 **When the law requires,** for example a valid court order.

6.5 We **never sell** consumer health data. We **never** use it for advertising or marketing, share it with data brokers, use it to decide eligibility, team selection, pricing, insurance or employment, use it for research, train any model with it, or send it to AI models.

## 7. How we protect it

Health values are encrypted before they are stored, in the United States, and never in iCloud. Access is limited to you and the people you share with. Logs and error reports never contain health values.

## 8. How long we keep it

- Adults: data older than 24 months is deleted automatically.
- Teens: data older than 12 months is deleted automatically.
- After 12 months without a sync, all Duna Health data is deleted.
- When you disconnect, withdraw consent or delete your account, data is deleted within 7 days, and leaves backups within 6 months.
- Records of consents and access are kept for 6 years.

## 9. Your rights

You may:

- confirm whether we hold your health data, see it, and get a list of everyone we shared it with (including the coach or club and when);
- delete it (disconnect in the app, or ask us);
- withdraw consent to collection or sharing;
- appeal a decision on your request.

Use the app, or email privacy@duna.coach. We respond within 45 days (and may extend once by 45 days where reasonably necessary, telling you why). We do not charge for requests. Guardians can make requests for teens. To appeal, reply to our decision; we respond within 45 days, and you may contact the Washington or Nevada Attorney General if you disagree.

## 10. Changes and contact

We will ask for your consent again before using health data in a new way. Contact privacy@duna.coach or [Duna mailing address].
