# Group sessions that fill up: player minimums and group-size pricing (PART)

Dated 28 November 2026. Work package PART-0: design and specification only. It picks up pull request 289 ("Add player minimums, group-size credit pricing and family booking for catalog sessions") and turns it into one capability that every group booking in Duna uses. Schema and contract requests are in [`schema-requests/PART.md`](schema-requests/PART.md). Code facts were read from `origin/main` at `6d9e1c3e`; paths are relative to the repository root.

## Owner brief

**Players and parents.** Every group session says where it stands ("Needs 2 more · confirms Thu 6:00 PM"), the price at each group size and the most they can pay. After booking they can help it fill (share a link, add a friend, book a child) and hear from Duna when it is confirmed or cancelled, with exactly what came back to their wallet or card. Nobody pays more than they agreed; nobody finds a cancelled clinic at the court.

**Coaches.** Their week shows sessions at risk, who is in, and three actions: nudge the group, run it anyway, ask the club to cancel. Attendance takes two taps and works offline.

**Owners.** One club sentence ("Runs with at least 3 players. If fewer are booked 24 hours before, cancel and return credits.") that every lesson inherits, four presets, a Today card of this week's at-risk sessions and the money attached, and confirmed interventions: extend, merge, run anyway, cancel with a message.

**Decisions (section 12, a minute each):** (1) the free-cancellation deadline and the group decision become one time, default 24 hours; (2) no-shows pay like everyone; price never depends on attendance; (3) price per player never rises as the group grows; (4) late cancellation stays, costs the final price, refunded if the place fills; (5) no booking the same session twice; (6) card payers get group pricing like credits; (7) old "auto-cancel" settings apply only to new sessions.

## 1. Principles

1. **One rule, said in one sentence.** A group rule is four facts: the smallest group that runs, the largest, when the group is decided, and what happens if it is too small. Everything a player, coach or owner sees is generated from those facts by one function, so the words never disagree between surfaces.
2. **One moment that matters.** The time the group is decided (the **lock**) is also the last moment to cancel for free. Players learn one time, not two.
3. **Never charge more than agreed.** The price a player sees at booking is the most they can pay. Every later change moves money back to them, never out of them.
4. **After the lock, numbers never cancel a session.** Only a person at the club can cancel a confirmed session, and they confirm the refunds first.
5. **A person confirms every message an operator triggers and every cancellation that moves money.** Automatic notices go only to people about their own booking.
6. **Help it happen, then tell the truth.** Before the lock the product helps the group fill (share, invite, waitlist). At the lock it says what happened, once, on the person's own channel.
7. **Fewer settings.** Presets and a club default cover most offerings. Every extra field must answer a real club question; "settlement basis" does not, so it goes (decision 2).
8. **Nothing changes under a booked player's feet.** A rule change that makes things worse for people who already booked never applies to them silently.

## 3. The model

### 3.1 Terms

- **Session**: one bookable occurrence (a catalog session occurrence, an event session, a pickup, a division's draw, a league season).
- **Booked**: confirmed places, counting group bookings by their quantity (`confirmedOccurrenceHeadcount`, `catalog-participation.ts:130`). Unpaid checkout holds take a place but do not count toward the minimum.
- **Needed**: `max(0, minimum − booked)`.
- **Lock**: start minus the lock minutes, moved later when the session opened inside its own window (`participationCutoffAt`, `participation.ts:359`).
- **Final group size**: booked at the lock. Bookings after the lock join at the final price and never change it.

### 3.2 States of a session with respect to its minimum

| State (`displayState`) | When                                                                        | Player sees                                            | Coach and club see                 | Exits                                                                                           |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `needs-players`        | Before the lock, booked < minimum                                           | "Needs 2 more" · "Confirms Thu 6:00 PM if 2 more join" | "At risk · needs 2 by Thu 6:00 PM" | booking → `minimum-reached`; lock → `cancelled-low` or `running-small`; club → `cancelled-club` |
| `minimum-reached`      | Before the lock, minimum ≤ booked < capacity                                | "Minimum reached · confirms Thu 6:00 PM"               | "On track · 4 of 12"               | cancellations → `needs-players`; full → `full`; lock → `confirmed`                              |
| `full`                 | Before or after the lock, booked = capacity                                 | "Full · join the waitlist"                             | "Full · 3 waiting"                 | cancellation → previous state and a waitlist offer                                              |
| `confirmed`            | Lock passed, minimum met                                                    | "Confirmed · 5 players"                                | "Confirmed · 5"                    | end → `done`; club → `cancelled-club`                                                           |
| `running-small`        | Lock passed, under minimum, rule says run (or an operator chose Run anyway) | "Confirmed · small group"                              | "Running with 2 of 4"              | end → `done`                                                                                    |
| `cancelled-low`        | Lock passed, under minimum, rule says cancel                                | "Cancelled · not enough players"                       | "Cancelled · 2 of 4 booked"        | terminal (refunds run until every booking is released)                                          |
| `cancelled-club`       | A person at the club cancelled (coach ill, weather, venue)                  | "Cancelled by {club}"                                  | "Cancelled by {name}"              | terminal                                                                                        |
| `done`                 | Ended; attendance open for 24 hours                                         | "Done" (+ "You were marked absent" if so)              | "Mark attendance" until marked     | terminal                                                                                        |

A session without a group rule shows only capacity ("3 spots left") and never enters these states.

### 3.3 State machine (text diagram)

```
                 book (booked >= min)
   ┌───────────────┐ ───────────────────────▶ ┌──────────────────┐   booked = capacity   ┌──────┐
   │ needs-players │ ◀─────────────────────── │ minimum-reached  │ ─────────────────────▶│ full │
   └───────────────┘   cancel (booked < min)  └──────────────────┘ ◀──────────────────── └──────┘
          │                                           │                cancel                │
          │ LOCK, rule = cancel                       │ LOCK                                  │ LOCK
          ▼                                           ▼                                       ▼
   ┌───────────────┐   LOCK, rule = run, or   ┌──────────────────┐                     ┌───────────┐
   │ cancelled-low │   operator "Run anyway"  │    confirmed     │ ◀───────────────────│ confirmed │
   └───────────────┘   ───────────────────▶   └──────────────────┘                     └───────────┘
                         ┌───────────────┐            │ end
                         │ running-small │ ───────────┤
                         └───────────────┘            ▼
                                               ┌──────────────┐  attendance or 24 h  ┌─────────┐
   any state before end ── club cancels ──▶    │     done     │ ────────────────────▶│ settled │
   cancelled-club (full refunds, confirmed)    └──────────────┘                      └─────────┘
```

Operator moves before the lock: **Extend lock** (stays in its state, new lock time), **Run anyway** (pre-decides `running-small` or `confirmed`), **Merge** (moves bookings into another session of the same offering, then `cancelled-club` with reason `merged` for the emptied one), **Cancel** (`cancelled-club`).

### 4.2 When it runs and idempotency

- **Cron** every 5 minutes (exists: `/api/cron/catalog-participation`, `processDueCatalogParticipation`, `catalog-participation.ts:1080`), widened to every subject type through the adapter.
- **On demand**: checkout decides first when the lock has passed (`catalog-checkout.ts:834-865`, keep); booking and cancellation recompute the display state and may emit `minimum-reached` notices; operator interventions call the engine directly.
- **Claims**: the decision is claimed with one conditional update (exists, `catalog-participation.ts:852-867`); settlement rows are unique per fulfilment (exists). New: notices are unique on (subject, person, kind) in the outbox; operator interventions carry an idempotency key (the existing `runIdempotentMutation`).
- **Never late-cancel**: a decision missed until after the start becomes `running-small` (exists, `catalog-participation.ts:847-849`; keep).

## 5. Money

### 5.1 How the price is explained (one line, then the ladder)

- Checkout, credits, tiers: **"3 credits now. If more join by Thu 6:00 PM, the difference comes back."** Under it, a three-row ladder: "1 player · 3 credits", "2 to 6 players · 2 credits each", "7 or more · 1 credit each", the current row marked "Now (5 booked)".
- Checkout, no tiers: no extra line; the price is the price.
- Receipt and wallet after the lock: **"Thursday clinic · paid 2 credits · 1 returned (group of 5)"**.
- HQ preview uses the same strings (section 7.1).

**Why not hold the card and capture at the lock?** Payments use automatic capture today (`payments.ts:2535`). A card authorisation hold is short (Stripe documents 7 days for most online card payments; verify the current limit before building), so sessions booked more than a week ahead would still need "charge, then refund". Two mechanisms for one promise is worse than one. **Why not charge at the lock?** It needs a saved card charged off-session; a decline after the lock leaves the club with a player and no payment. Reserve-then-return is the one mechanism that works for every booking horizon and both methods.

**Costs of the card version.** The processing fee is not returned on a partial refund; the payout policy proposes the club bears it (`payout-policy.md` §6). Taxes: the refund must reverse tax in proportion; verify `tax-policy.ts` handles partial refunds before PART-10 (**Needs API** if not).

### 5.3 Rounding, tiers and fees

- Credits are whole numbers; tiers are per player, so there is nothing to split. Money tiers are whole minor units per player.
- "Split the court": per-player share rounded up; the remainder is shown on the host's receipt ("Court 60.00 · 4 players · 15.00 each").
- Service Fee: computed on the final price with the existing pricing rules (`packages/pricing/src/index.ts` rounds with `Math.round` per order).
- Tiers must not increase per player (decision 3); total-revenue drops are warnings with a suggested fix.

### 5.4 Payout: delivery releases, a cancelled session releases nothing

- Rule for PART-10, aligned with `payout-policy.md` P1: a card payment for a session with a group rule is **never releasable before the lock is decided and the session has ended**. `cancelled-low` and `cancelled-club` release nothing; the refund comes from the held funds.
- **Today's gap (contradiction):** release time is the session start minus the refund window (`money-service.ts:313-350`). With a lock shorter than the refund window (for example lock 6 hours, refund window 24 hours), a card payment becomes releasable and can be paid out by the hourly payout cron before the session is cancelled under its minimum, so the refund then comes from a paid-out balance. Decision 1 removes most of the gap; PART-10 closes it.
- Credits: paid credit packs are released on settlement with an Unredeemed Credit Reserve (`payout-policy.md` note D). Returned credits become unredeemed again, so the reserve must be recalculated from the wallet ledger, not from bookings (note for P8).

## 12. Owner decisions

Each: the recommendation, why, and what the alternative costs.

1. **Default lock time (today 24 hours).** Recommend: the lock **is** the club's free-cancellation deadline, default 24 hours (proposed), editable per offering. Why: players remember one time; it removes the case where someone cancels free after a session was confirmed, or forfeits before it was decided. Alternative (two separate times) costs confused players and the payout gap in section 5.4.
2. **No-shows under attendance pricing.** Recommend: remove attendance-based pricing; the price is fixed at the lock and no-shows pay the same as everyone. Attendance is still recorded for coaches and reports. Why: under attendance pricing one no-show raises everyone else's price after the session; today's code also charges the no-show. Alternative costs a rule nobody can explain at checkout.
3. **Must tiers get cheaper as groups grow?** Recommend: yes, per player price may never rise; Duna also warns when one more player lowers the club's total. Why: "invite a friend" must never cost the player more. Alternative lets a club punish growth by accident.
4. **Late cancellation with acknowledgement.** Recommend: keep it (already built: `acknowledgeNoRefund`), but charge the **final** per-player price, not the reserve, and return it if someone takes the place. Why: today a late canceller pays the maximum, more than anyone who played. Alternative (no late cancellation) leaves people who can't come holding a place a waitlisted player wants.
5. **Block a second booking of the same session for the same person.** Recommend: block it; "Add a player" is the way to bring guests. Why: a double tap or a parent and child both booking the child silently doubles the charge. Alternative costs refunds and support.
6. **Group pricing for card payers.** Recommend: same as credits: charge the most, refund the difference at the lock. Cost: the club bears the card fee on the refunded part; the refund takes days to appear. Alternative: card pays a fixed price (today), so card and credit players in the same clinic pay differently.
7. **Old "auto-cancel" settings that never worked.** Recommend: honour them only for sessions created from now on; existing ones run and owners get a review list. Alternative: switching them on would cancel sessions people already booked expecting them to run.

## 14. Worked examples

All numbers are illustrative credit amounts, not recommended prices.

**1. Semi-private lesson.** Preset Semi-private: minimum 2, capacity 2, lock 24 hours, cancel if too small, price 4 credits each (no ladder). Maya books Saturday 9:00 AM on Wednesday. Her booking card: "Needs 1 more · confirms Fri 9:00 AM if 1 more joins". She shares the link; Jordan books Thursday: chip "Minimum reached". Friday 9:00 AM: both get "Saturday semi-private is on · 2 players". _Other branch:_ nobody joins. Thursday 9:00 AM Maya gets "needs 1 more" (24 hours before the lock). Friday 9:00 AM: "cancelled · only 1 of the 2 players it needs booked. 4 credits are back in your wallet." The coach's Pro shows the hour free. If the director had pressed Run anyway on Thursday, Maya would pay 4 credits for what became a private lesson; she is never charged more than she agreed.

**2. Twelve-person clinic with a ladder.** Preset Clinic: minimum 4, capacity 12, lock 24 hours, cancel if too small; ladder 4–7 players · 3 credits each, 8–12 players · 2 credits each. HQ warns: "An 8th player lowers your total from 21 to 16 credits"; the owner keeps it to fill the clinic. Checkout: "3 credits now. If more join by Wed 6:00 PM, the difference comes back." 9 people book (one parent books 2 for twins). At the lock: 9 → tier 2. Each place settles at 2 credits; 1 credit per place returns (the parent gets 2). Total collected 18 credits. A 10th person books after the lock and pays 2. One player cancels after the lock: today they would keep paying 3; proposed, they pay 2, and get it back when the waitlisted 11th person takes the place. One no-show pays 2 and is marked absent.

**3. Pickup that needs 4.** A player hosts Saturday 8:00 AM pickup: capacity 6, minimum 4, decide 12 hours before (one of the host's existing choices), cancel if too small, free. At 8:00 PM Friday (the lock) only 3 have joined → "Saturday pickup is cancelled · only 3 of the 4 players it needs joined." Nobody pays, nobody drives to the beach. _Other branch:_ a 4th joins from the host's link at 7:30 PM → at 8:00 PM all four get "Saturday pickup is on · 4 players". Two more join; a 7th person joins the waitlist. When one player cancels at 7:00 AM Saturday (after the lock; a free pickup has nothing to charge), the waitlisted player gets "A place opened · join by 7:45 AM" (the hold never runs past the start). If the host had chosen to split a 60.00 court, each of 4 would pay 15.00; with 5 the share would be 12.00 and the difference returns as in example 2 (PART-9 with PART-10).

## 15. What today's code says that 289's description does not

- Late cancellation with acknowledgement is listed as an open decision but is built (`cancelCatalogSessionBooking`, `acknowledgeNoRefund`, `catalog-session-bookings.ts:1036-1043`); the late canceller keeps paying the full reserve.
- No-show charging is listed as open but is built: under attendance pricing every confirmed booking, present or not, settles at the attended group's tier (`catalog-participation.ts:1039-1063`).
- "Refund cannot refund a credit-paid order" is true only at the router (`amountMinor` must be positive, `router.ts:12156`); the service already restores credits for a zero-amount credit order (`catalog-service.ts:8190-8253`), which is how automatic under-minimum refunds work.
- Only tiers are snapshotted on bookings; the minimum, policy and lock are read live from the offering, so editing an offering changes sessions people already booked.
- The lock and the refund window are independent settings (`catalog-session-bookings.ts:81-107`), and fund release uses the refund window (section 5.4).
- "Auto-cancel low attendance" for events is not stored on any column; it sits inside `event_blueprints.registration_settings.smartRules` JSON. Pickup's version tells hosts that Duna checks, which it does not.
- Capacity counts unpaid holds while the minimum counts only confirmed places, so "spots left" and "needs n" can disagree during a checkout.
- HQ's "Players per booking" is the session capacity.
