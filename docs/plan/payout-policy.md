# Duna Pay payout and holdback policy: engineering spec

Status: draft, 27 November 2026. It is the basis for `apps/web/content/legal/duna-pay.md` §5–§9 and for the engineering gap list in `docs/legal/README.md` (d). Not legal advice; every default marked **(owner)** is an owner decision and every point marked **(counsel)** needs a lawyer.

Code facts below were read from `origin/main` at `a11b21a`. Paths are relative to the repository root.

## 1. How money moves today (the facts this policy must fit)

- Organizations hold Stripe Accounts v2 connected accounts with the Express dashboard, the merchant `card_payments` and recipient `stripe_transfers` configurations, and `fees_collector` and `losses_collector` both set to `application` (`packages/api/src/payments.ts:1873-1911`). Duna, the platform, is therefore responsible to Stripe for Stripe fees, refunds, disputes and negative balances.
- Every player payment is a destination charge. `transfer_data.destination` is always set and `on_behalf_of` is added only once the organization's `card_payments` capability is active (`payments.ts:52-63`, `packages/api/src/stripe-connect.ts:23-41`). The organization is the settlement merchant only when `on_behalf_of` is set.
- Duna takes one `application_fee_amount` per charge. It equals the player Service Fee plus an estimated processing cost (online card 3% + $0.35, card present 2.8% + $0.15, ACH 1% capped at $6) plus the Organization Fee, capped at the order total (`packages/pricing/src/index.ts:200-292`, `packages/api/src/catalog-checkout.ts:1166-1220`, `packages/api/src/checkout.ts:2169-2174`).
- The connected account's Stripe payout schedule is forced to `manual` (`payments.ts:2286-2320`). Duna creates payouts itself from an hourly cron (`apps/hq/vercel.json`, `apps/hq/app/api/cron/money-payouts`, `packages/api/src/money-service.ts:1602-1782`).
- Each charge gets a row in `payment_fund_schedules` with status `pending-clearance`, `held`, `available`, `payout-pending`, `paid-out`, `partially-refunded`, `refunded` or `disputed` (`packages/db/src/schema.ts:9370-9436`). The release time is the later of Stripe's `available_on` and a policy release time: order creation for non-refundable items, otherwise the event **start** minus the refund window, or order creation plus the window (default 24 hours) when there is no event (`money-service.ts:313-350`). Memberships use `refundWindowDays`, default 7 (`packages/api/src/workflows.ts:1255-1262`).
- `organization_money_settings` stores `payout_interval` (manual, daily, weekly by default, monthly), weekday, monthly day and `minimum_payout_minor` (default 0) (`schema.ts:9304-9360`). The HQ screen offers only weekly, monthly and manual and shows a saved "daily" as weekly (`apps/hq/components/money-workspace.tsx:419-423, 940-962`).
- There is no reserve, first-payout delay, negative-balance recovery, set-off, closure handling or dispute ledger journal.
- Stripe limits: reserves on connected accounts may last at most 180 days, and Stripe requires the platform's terms to explain its reserve policy (<https://docs.stripe.com/connect/connected-account-reserves>). Manual payouts may hold US funds for up to 2 years (<https://docs.stripe.com/connect/manual-payouts>). Stripe does not provide escrow, so the policy never uses that word.

## 2. Definitions

- **Business day**: Monday to Friday, excluding US federal bank holidays.
- **Settled**: Stripe reports the funds for the charge as available (`available_on` has passed). Card payments typically settle in about 2 business days; ACH can take longer.
- **Delivered**: the condition in §4 for the product type has occurred.
- **Releasable**: settled, delivered, and not subject to a hold in §5.
- **Payout**: Duna instructs Stripe to send releasable funds from the organization's connected-account balance to its bank account. Arrival time after the instruction is set by Stripe and the bank.
- **Organization Proceeds**: the amount of a charge transferred to the organization's connected account, which is the charge total minus Duna's application fee and minus sales tax that Duna collects as the seller of record for tax (see `packages/api/src/tax-policy.ts:78-83` and `payments.ts:1368-1410`).

## 3. Principle

Funds for a sale are released to the organization when (a) the payment has settled and (b) the thing sold has been delivered. Duna then pays them out within **3 business days** on the daily schedule, or on the next scheduled payout day for weekly or monthly schedules, subject to the organization's minimum payout amount and to the holds in §5.

**Change from the starting position:** the brief said "within 3 days of both being true, on the organization's chosen payout schedule". Those two conflict when the organization picks weekly or monthly. The spec makes **daily** the default schedule, so the 3-business-day promise holds by default, and states plainly that weekly and monthly are slower by choice.

Duna's own revenue (Duna Premium, the player Service Fee, organization plan fees, video packs, the website add-on) is never part of Organization Proceeds and is outside this table.

**Note A: multi-day events at the end, not per day.** Refund and dispute risk on an event is concentrated in cancellation or non-delivery of the event as a whole (weather, a venue loss, an organizer failing to run later rounds), and players buy a single entry rather than a day. Releasing per day would pay out an entry fee for a tournament that is then abandoned on day two. Seven days covers weekend tournaments and week-long camps scheduled as events; anything longer behaves like a program so cash is not tied up for weeks.

**Note B: programs, camps and leagues.**

| Option                                          | Club cash flow                                      | Player protection                                             | Engineering                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Pro-rata by session (starting position)         | Smooth, matches delivery                            | Strong: undelivered sessions are still held if the club stops | Needs session schedule on the fund schedule; weekly fallback |
| First tranche at start (e.g. 25%) then pro-rata | Better: covers set-up costs (permits, balls, staff) | Weaker by the tranche                                         | Small addition to pro-rata                                   |
| Monthly tranches                                | Lumpy, slower for short programs                    | Medium                                                        | Simplest, but misaligned with delivery                       |

Recommendation: pro-rata by session (weekly fallback) as the rule, plus an **optional start tranche of up to 25%** released when the first session ends, available only to organizations with at least 90 days of payouts, a refund rate under 5% and a dispute rate under 0.75% over the last 90 days **(owner: the thresholds)**. Reason: clubs incur most program costs before the first session; the tranche is small enough that a club stopping mid-program still leaves most of the undelivered value held.

**Note C: memberships.** A membership with a `full-within-window` refund behaviour (`packages/core/src/membership-subscription-policy.ts`) promises a full refund for up to 30 days. Releasing after 3 business days would force a refund out of future payouts or the organization's bank. The release therefore waits for the later of settlement + 3 business days and the end of that window. Plans with `none` or `prorated` refund behaviour release after settlement + 3 business days.

**Note D: credit packs.**

| Option                                                          | Club expectation                           | Player protection if club closes           | Notes                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release on settlement (cash up front)                           | Met                                        | None beyond Duna's general recovery rights | Clubs sell packs to raise cash now                        |
| Release as credits are redeemed                                 | Not met; a 20-session pack may take a year | Full                                       | Creates large, long holds; up to Stripe's 2-year US limit |
| Release on settlement with a reserve on unredeemed paid credits | Mostly met                                 | Partial, sized by the reserve              | Recommended                                               |

Recommendation: release on settlement + 3 business days, and hold an **Unredeemed Credit Reserve of 20%** of the cash value of unredeemed paid credits, recalculated daily and released as credits are redeemed or refunded **(owner: 20%)**. For an organization's first 90 days, paid credit packs release on redemption only. Reason: clubs get most of the cash immediately; the reserve funds refunds to players if a club closes or is suspended, which is the realistic failure. Membership-benefit and promotional credits carry no cash and need no reserve.

**Note E: goods.** The FTC Mail, Internet or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule (16 CFR 435) requires a reasonable basis for any stated shipping time, otherwise shipment within 30 days, delay notices with a cancel option, and refunds within 7 working days of cancellation (<https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/business-guide-ftcs-mail-internet-or-telephone-order-merchandise-rule>). The policy therefore requires the organization to state a ship or pick-up time; if none is stated, 30 days applies, after which the buyer may cancel for a full refund from held funds. Returns follow the organization's posted returns policy, with a floor of a full refund for items that are defective, not as described or not delivered.

## 5. Holds and holdbacks

### 5.1 Disputes

- When a dispute opens, Duna places a hold on the organization's balance for the disputed amount plus the dispute fee Stripe charges. Because charges are destination charges, Stripe debits the **platform** for the dispute; Duna recovers it from the organization by reversing the transfer for the disputed amount (Stripe docs: <https://docs.stripe.com/connect/destination-charges>).
- The organization supplies evidence through HQ within 7 days of notice **(owner)**, before Stripe's `due_by`. Duna submits evidence. Duna may accept a dispute it judges unwinnable after telling the organization.
- If the dispute is won, the hold is released and the reversed amount transferred back; the dispute fee is returned only if Stripe returns it. If lost, the amount and fee remain with the organization.
- **Code today: partly.** `synchronizeMoneyDispute` stores the dispute and marks the fund schedule `disputed`, restoring on a win (`money-service.ts:1863-1941`). There is no transfer reversal, fee handling, ledger journal or evidence submission, and HQ lists disputes from the connected account (`payments.ts:2099, 2267-2277`) where destination-charge disputes do not appear.

### 5.2 Refunds

- Refunds come from, in order: the held funds of that sale; the organization's available balance; future proceeds; then the organization's bank account (see 5.5).
- **Code today: partly.** Catalog refunds reverse the transfer (`reverse_transfer: true`, `packages/api/src/catalog-service.ts:8335-8342`). After a payout there is no recovery path beyond Stripe's connected-account negative balance.

### 5.3 First-payout delay

- New organizations: first payout no earlier than 7 days after the first successful charge **(owner: 7)**, in addition to the delivery rule. Waived for organizations that Duna has reviewed and approved.
- **Code today: no.**

### 5.4 Reserves

- Duna may hold a **rolling reserve** of a percentage of Organization Proceeds for a number of days, with written notice stating the reason, percentage, duration and review date. Triggers: the organization's first 90 days if its expected volume is high **(owner)**; a 90-day refund rate above 10% or dispute rate above 0.75%; sales delivered more than 90 days after purchase; a material change in business; a Stripe requirement.
- Default when triggered: 10% for 90 days **(owner)**. Maximum duration 180 days (Stripe's limit). Reviewed at least every 30 days; the organization may ask for review at any time.
- The Unredeemed Credit Reserve (Note D) is a standing reserve, not a triggered one.
- **Code today: no.**

### 5.5 Negative balances and recovery

- A negative balance arises when refunds, disputes or fees exceed what is held. Duna recovers it by (1) set-off against future proceeds, (2) debiting the organization's bank account through Stripe where the connected-account agreement allows (`debit_negative_balances`), and (3) invoicing the organization, payable in 15 days **(owner)**.
- Duna may set off any amount the organization owes Duna (plan fees, video overages, the website add-on, negative balances) against proceeds.
- **Code today: no.**

### 5.6 Suspension and closure

- On suspension or closure, Duna stops new sales, then: (1) the organization delivers or cancels-and-refunds outstanding bookings, programs and unfulfilled orders; (2) held funds pay those refunds and any refunds of unredeemed paid credits players request within 60 days of notice **(owner)**; (3) the remainder is released after 120 days (the typical card-dispute window), less any negative balance and amounts owed.
- **Code today: no.**
